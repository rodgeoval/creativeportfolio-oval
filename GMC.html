Gryffindor Medical Center

GMC

Gryffindor Medical Center is a leading healthcare facility which appreciates the values of audacity, resolve, and gallantry. It’s medical personnel, otherwise called ‘Gryffindors’ reflect the readiness of the center to deal with acute medical concerns.

"Gryffindor" as a name comes directly from Godric Gryffindor. The medical center aims to preserve his name by attempting to create an atmosphere that is favorable for medical innovation. The emblem of Gryffindor Medical Center contains a lion, one of the universal symbols of courage and bravery. It is a testimony to the center’s commitment to bold change and advocacy for patients. The center’s branding of gold and scarlet vividly portrays the passionate and relentless drive to achieve unparalleled standards of clinical excellence.

Core Features

Scope

The Gryffindor Medical Center system focuses on six modules: Patient Management, Staff Management, Appointment Management, Facility Management, Inventory Management, and Accounting Management. The system includes built-in security features to ensure that all users, whether administrative or medical personnel, have log-in credentials. Each user will have a maximum of 3 login attempts to access their respective modules.

Limitation

It is only designed for internal use only and does not support remote access outside the Gryffindor Medical Center.
