NU-IRS

This application allows students at National University – MOA to file reports for items they have lost or found by filling out a form that will be posted for other users to see. The app has both Admin and User interfaces. Admins are responsible for reviewing submitted forms and may either approve or reject them. Once a form is approved, it becomes visible to all users. Users can submit reports and choose from the category button for missing items. The form includes details such as the user's name, item name, description, location, and time. Items are categorized as valuables, non-valuables, and perishable items.

Overall, the application improves the efficiency of the processes and reduces the confusion, helping the National University – MOA community a lot more when it comes to the coordination of the lost and found items.
