PAYBEELZzz

It aims to simplify utility payments by providing customers and administrators with a secure and planned systematic method of paying utility bills. Service providers also benefit from accurately tracking bills and organized documentation, while Customers benefit from a convenient payment process that saves time and keeps the lines organized.

Overall, The PAYBEELZzz Queueing Management System (QMS) is a system that gives more efficient usage of time, saves operational cost, and improves the overall system.

Core Features

Scope

Our system functionalities begins with the Admin Login and an Admin Dashboard, which serves as the control panel. For customers, it starts with Customer Inquiry to provide payment details, followed by the Queue Number. The only features are Queueing Management and Recent transaction under Admin Dashboard, which allows the admin to actively manage the service line using buttons such as Call, Recall, Remove, and mark every transactions as Paid. Lastly Admin and customers can View Queue.

Limitations

Our system is limited to simple transactions, such as Customer Inquiry for customers, Queueing Management and Recent Transaction History for admin user. The system does not include any other features such as Staff Availability, Customer Feedback, Online Payment Transactions, Digital Billing Websites, Official Payment Receipt, etc. It just focuses on the queueing itself for conveniency and organization of customer’s billing.
